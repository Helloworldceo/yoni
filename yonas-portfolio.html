About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU), conducting advanced research on 2D materials, molecular electronics, and nanoelectronic device engineering with a focus on interfacial phenomena and charge transport.

My work spans designing molecular junctions and graphene-based devices for sensing, energy, and next-generation electronics. I supervise PhD students, manage research proposals, and collaborate with international teams on semiconductor devices and nanoscale characterization.

Previously I held postdoctoral positions at GIST (South Korea) and Sungkyunkwan University, and have been a Lecturer at Addis Ababa University for over a decade.
