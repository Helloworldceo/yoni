About Me

Research Excellence in
Nanotechnology

Bridging fundamental science and applied engineering to develop next-generation electronic devices and sensing systems.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Advanced Materials Research Center (AMRC), Centre of Excellence for Molecular Innovation and Advanced Materials, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU). My research integrates 2D-material synthesis and transfer, nanofabrication, molecular electronics, quantum transport, semiconductor devices, and interfacial mechanics to understand structure–property relationships at the nanoscale.

My trajectory has progressed from adhesion, cohesion, and interfacial mechanics of 2D materials to 2D semiconductor devices, molecular junctions, quantum interference, and advanced sensing — spanning postdoctoral appointments at Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST) and Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), where I earned my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering.

My long-term goal is to establish an independent research program developing molecularly engineered 2D interfaces for quantum electronics, low-power nanoelectronics, and intelligent sensing — while mentoring the next generation of researchers.

2D Materials
CVD synthesis and transfer of graphene, MoS₂, WS₂, WSe₂, and 2D heterostructures for nanoelectronic devices.
Molecular Electronics
Single-molecule junctions, molecular conductance, and mechanically/electrically controlled electronic states.
Quantum Transport
Quantum interference and nanoscale charge transport across molecular and 2D-material interfaces.
AI-Enabled Materials
Data-driven and AI-assisted materials research integrated with intelligent sensing platforms.
